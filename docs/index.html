Welcome to Premalaya Social Development Society

Premalaya Social Development Society works for the well-being of downtrodden and vulnerable sections in Tiruvallur, Chennai Districts of Tamilnadu and in Prakasam, Nellore Districts of Andhra Pradesh. We work for the community through education, health, advocacy, communication and social mobilization.
