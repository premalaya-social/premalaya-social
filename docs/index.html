Welcome to Premalaya Social Development Society

Premalaya Social Development Society works for the well-being of downtrodden and vulnerable sections in Tiruvallur, Chennai Districts of Tamilnadu and in Prakasam, Nellore Districts of Andhra Pradesh. We work for the community through education, health, advocacy, communication and social mobilization.

What We Do

Our Programs

Women Empowerment

Premalaya trains women in tailoring, computer skills, bag making and other valuable skills for economic independence.

Children Home

Premalaya Children has taken care of orphans and semi-orphans, providing them with care, education and hope.

Health Care

Premalaya provides basic health care services for the benefit of society, ensuring wellness for all.

Relief Operations

In times of natural calamities, Premalaya has provided manpower and material support to affected communities.

Educational Activities

Premalaya has been instrumental in sending a number of children to school, ensuring access to quality education.

Medical Camps

Premalaya conducts periodical medical camps in villages, slums and cities, bringing healthcare to those who need it most.
