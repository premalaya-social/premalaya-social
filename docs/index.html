How We Serve

We are committed to creating lasting change through dedicated programs in education, health, and community development.

Women Empowerment

Premalaya equips women with practical skills such as tailoring, computer training, and other livelihood-oriented activities to promote economic independence.

Learn More

Children Home

Premalaya Children has taken care of orphans and semi-orphans, providing them with care, education and hope.

Learn More

Health Care

Premalaya provides basic health care services for the benefit of society, ensuring wellness for all.

Learn More

Relief Operations

In times of natural calamities, Premalaya has provided manpower and material support to affected communities.

Learn More

Educational Activities

Premalaya has been instrumental in sending a number of children to school, ensuring access to quality education.
