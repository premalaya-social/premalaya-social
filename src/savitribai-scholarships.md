## Scholarships for Deserving Students

Our Savitribai Scholarships support students who have the ability and the drive to study, but not the means. The scholarship helps cover the cost of education so that financial hardship does not stand in the way of a bright student's future.

### About the Scholarship

- Financial assistance for deserving and needy students
- Support towards the cost of education
- Awarded in the name of Savitribai Phule, a pioneer of women's education in India
