## Scholarships for Deserving Students

Our Savitribai Phule Scholarships support students who have the ability and drive to study but lack the means. The scholarship helps cover the cost of education so that financial hardship does not stand in the way of a bright student's future.

### Why It Matters

For many talented students from low-income families, the cost of school fees, books, and materials is the only thing standing between them and an education. When families cannot afford these costs, promising students drop out and their potential is lost. A scholarship keeps them in school.

### About the Scholarship

- Financial assistance for deserving students in need
- Support toward the cost of education, books, and materials
- Awarded in the name of Savitribai Phule, a pioneer of women's education in India
- Open to students from low-income families who are at risk of dropping out
