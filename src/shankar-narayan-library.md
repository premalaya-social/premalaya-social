## A Library Open to All

The Shankar Narayan Memorial Library provides free access to books and learning resources for students and residents in and around the Premalaya office. It offers a quiet place to study and a wider world of books beyond the classroom.

### Why It Matters

Many students in the communities we serve have no quiet place to study and no books beyond their school textbooks. First-generation learners, in particular, have few places to read, explore new ideas, or prepare for exams. A free, welcoming library gives them the space and the resources to go further.

### What We Offer

- Free access to books and learning resources
- A quiet, welcoming space for reading and study
- Reference and study materials for school and competitive exams
- Support and encouragement for young learners and first-generation readers
- A community space that nurtures a lasting habit of reading
