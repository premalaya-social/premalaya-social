## Our Key Focus Areas

### Education & Child Development

> “A child without education is like a bird without wings.”

We support children from slum and marginalized communities by improving access to education, care, and essential learning resources.

- Supplementary education for school-going children and dropouts in slum and marginalized communities
- Distribution of books, uniforms, bags, and other educational materials
- Day Care Centers that provide safety, care, and nutrition for young children

### Savitribai Phule Scholarships

Our scholarship program supports students from economically disadvantaged backgrounds, helping them continue their education without financial barriers.

- Annual scholarships for deserving girl students and economically disadvantaged boys, covering school and college fees
- Many students have benefited from this program, enabling them to continue their education with dignity and hope
