## Building Skills, Building Dignity

We help women in our communities gain the skills and support they need to earn a living and stand on their own feet. Through self-help groups, training, and practical assistance, women move toward greater independence and dignity.

### Why It Matters

Many women in the communities we serve are confined to unpaid work at home, with little income of their own and few chances to learn a trade. With a skill, a sewing machine, and a supportive group of women around them, they can earn from home, support their families, and find a stronger voice of their own.

### What We Do

- Form Self-Help Groups (SHGs) that build savings, solidarity, and confidence
- Run free tailoring and skills training centers
- Distribute sewing machines free of cost to help women start earning
- Provide monthly groceries and clothing support for widows and vulnerable women
- Conduct awareness programs on women's rights

### Our Goal

That every woman we work with has the skills, support, and confidence to earn a living and live with dignity.
