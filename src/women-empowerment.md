## Building Skills, Building Dignity

We help women in our communities gain the skills and support they need to earn a living and stand on their own feet. Through self-help groups, training, and practical assistance, women move toward greater independence and dignity.

### What We Do

- Form Self-Help Groups (SHGs) that build savings and solidarity
- Run free tailoring training centres
- Distribute sewing machines free of cost
- Provide monthly groceries and clothing support for widows and vulnerable women
- Conduct awareness programmes on women's rights

### How You Can Help

Your support helps a woman build a better future for herself and her family. [Donate](/support/) or [contact us](/contact/) to learn more.
