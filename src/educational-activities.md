## Helping Every Child Learn

> A child without education is like a bird without wings.

In the communities we serve, many children — especially school dropouts — miss out on the education that could change their lives. Our educational activities give them a second chance to learn and stay in school.

### What We Do

- Provide supplementary education for school-going children and dropouts in slum and marginalized communities
- Distribute books, uniforms, bags, and educational materials
- Run Day Care Centres that give young children safety, care, and nutrition

### How You Can Help

You can help a child stay in school. [Donate](/support/) or [contact us](/contact/) to learn more.
